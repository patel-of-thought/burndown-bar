Inference

Spend you can see.
Limits you can set.

Connect OpenRouter with a Management key to see account credits, runway, and per-key spend next to your subscriptions.

Set a USD cap, pause or resume a key, and check Zero Data Retention guardrails without opening a browser.

Rankings

Which models
developers actually use.

OpenRouter rankings show top models and apps by tokens processed, today, this week, or this month.

Artificial Analysis compares the top 12 models by Intelligence Index, time per task, and cost per task.

Model Advisor

A recommendation
for the task.

Pick a decision model (Jev, OpenAI Decisions API etc. ) and a task, then get advice.

The recommendation weighs pricing, cheapness, intelligence, and your token usage. It uses your OpenRouter Standard API key, and the result is advisory.

Connections

CLIProxyAPI or direct.
Your choice.

Read Codex, Grok, and Claude through the accounts already signed in to CLIProxyAPI, or connect each one directly. Switch them together or one at a time under Quota source.

Every other provider connects on its own. Credentials you save stay in the macOS Keychain.
